1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, placing an order for a timepiece, subscribing to our digital catalog, or reaching out to customer support.

Personal Identifiers Full name, shipping address, email, and telephone number.
Transaction Data Watch preferences, order history, and encrypted payment confirmations.

Device and browser diagnostic telemetry are also passively logged to optimize your experience across desktop and mobile storefronts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Every piece of data collected serves a precise purpose dedicated to elevating your luxury experience with Alberto:

  • Processing secure purchases and dispatching curated timepiece shipments.
  • Customizing your customer dashboard and managing product wishlists.
  • Providing VIP notifications regarding limited collection drops and private exhibitions.
  • Enhancing website performance, responsive layout rendering, and security measures.

3. Data Security & Protection

We deploy robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

All financial checkouts are processed via industry-standard SSL encryption layers, ensuring complete confidentiality for your banking records and transactions.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. Data is shared strictly with trusted logistics partners (e.g., DHL, FedEx for watch delivery) under stringent confidentiality agreements solely to fulfill your orders.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes cookies and local browser storage to remember your dark/light theme choice, maintain cart status, and evaluate site traffic statistics securely. You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies at any time, though certain features like your shopping bag may require them.

6. Your User Rights

As a valued client, you retain absolute authority over your personal information. You hold the right to:

  • Request access to a complete export of your stored personal profile data.
  • Modify or correct inaccurate billing and shipping particulars instantly.
  • Request permanent account deletion and removal from all marketing registries.
