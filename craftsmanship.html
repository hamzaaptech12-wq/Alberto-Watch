The Art of Detail

Every Detail Tells a Story.

A fine watch brings together design, materials and engineering on a remarkably small scale. From the shape of its case to the finish of its dial, every element contributes to its character.

At Alberto, we celebrate the traditions and techniques that make timepieces fascinating to wear, admire and collect.

Beauty in the Smallest Details

Finishing Makes the Difference.

The texture of a dial, the polish of a case, the feel of a crown and the alignment of the hands all influence the experience of a watch.

Traditional decorative techniques can include brushing, polishing, engraving and patterned movement finishing. The exact methods vary between watchmakers and models.
