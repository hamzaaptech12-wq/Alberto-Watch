Electronic Innovation

The Rhythm of Quartz.

Quartz watches use a battery or another electrical power source to energize a tiny quartz crystal. Its consistent vibrations provide a precise reference for the timekeeping circuit.

Quartz technology is valued for its practicality, accuracy and relatively low maintenance. Solar-powered quartz models can recharge using light.

Connected Timekeeping

Intelligence on Your Wrist.

Smartwatches bring notifications, activity tracking, customizable faces and other digital features to a compact wearable device. Available functions depend on the model, operating system and connected phone.

They complement modern lifestyles while offering a different experience from traditional mechanical watches.
