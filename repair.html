The Right Care For Your Watch

Every Detail Matters.

Choose the service that best describes what your timepiece needs. Our team can confirm the appropriate repair after reviewing the watch and its condition.

Battery Replacement

For quartz watches that have stopped or are losing power. Battery and seal checks may be recommended after inspection.

Movement Servicing

Careful assessment of timekeeping, winding and movement concerns for mechanical, automatic and quartz timepieces.

Glass & Crystal

Support for scratched, cracked or damaged watch glass, with replacement options checked against the watch model.

Strap & Bracelet

Strap replacement, bracelet adjustment and help with worn or damaged fastening components.

Water-Resistance Check

Inspection for watches exposed to moisture. Water resistance should be tested rather than assumed from appearance alone.

Care & Restoration

Assessment of external wear, case condition and other concerns where a timepiece may benefit from detailed care.
