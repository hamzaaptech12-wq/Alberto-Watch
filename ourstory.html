Who We Are

Time Is Personal.

Our story began with a simple moment. As a young watch enthusiast, Alberto discovered an old mechanical wristwatch among his grandfather’s belongings. Its worn strap, softly ticking movement and carefully engraved case sparked a curiosity that would stay with him for years.

He began studying different movements, visiting watch repair workshops and collecting timepieces with distinctive stories. Over time, that personal fascination grew into an idea: create a place where people could discover watches not simply by price or trend, but by character, design and the memories they might one day hold.

Every second has a story.

What Drives Us

An Eye for the Extraordinary.

With the support of a small group of friends who shared his passion for design and technology, Alberto turned his idea into Alberto Watch Company. The first concept was modest: a carefully presented selection of classic and vintage-inspired watches, supported by helpful information about the craftsmanship behind them.

As interest grew, the vision expanded. Luxury, smart and contemporary collections joined the original selection, while educational pages about movements, maintenance and watchmaking became part of the experience. What began as a personal fascination became a brand story centered on discovery.

Beyond the Collection

A Relationship That Continues.

Alberto never wanted the relationship with a customer to end at the moment of purchase. His grandfather’s old watch had taught him that care, understanding and attention can keep a timepiece meaningful for years.

That idea continues to guide the company’s journey: share useful guidance, explain different watch technologies and make it easier for every customer to appreciate the watch they choose.
